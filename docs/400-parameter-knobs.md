# Parameter Knobs

**Status: design agreed 2026-11-01 (decision 3 changed and decision 14 added the same day, after hand-testing phase 1: a right-click assigns, a selected knob's `Delete` removes the mapping); phase 1 built 2026-11-01 (`feature/parameter-knobs`), phase 2 built 2026-11-04 (`feature/parameter-knob-capture`), phase 3 built 2026-11-04 (`feature/parameter-knobs-clap`). Issue: #16.** The knob panel's eight knobs (`390`) stop sending a MIDI CC and drive a plugin parameter instead, chosen per knob by turning the control in the plugin's own editor. The link goes both ways: turning the control in the editor moves Stev's knob, and the turn is captured with `/` like a knob drag. Recorded moves play back to the parameter, and each knob keeps its lane. The CC knobs are removed. A CC target comes back with the hardware-controller work (§ Out of scope). Read `030` § Knob Panel and `380` § The knob lanes (the panel and lane rules this keeps; their reasoning, and the `390` decisions cited below, are in `archive/390-knob-panel.md`), `370` (the landing rule), `130` (the plugin host) and `180` (the VST3 module) first.

## Background

From a discussion on 2026-11-01. `390` assumed a plugin maps a CC to its parameters itself, through fixed CCs or a MIDI learn, so Stev only had to send the right CC number (`390` § Background). Logging each VST3 plugin's `IMidiMapping` table at load (`ea79008`) showed that this doesn't hold for the plugins in use:

- **Spire's VST3 maps no CC to its macros.** It maps only 12 controllers (`180` § `kResultOk` does not mean "mapped"), none of them CC 14–17. A VST3 plugin hears a CC only through `IMidiMapping`, so the CC knobs never reached Spire's macros. `390`'s "Spire's macros listen on CC 14–17" was wrong for the VST3.
- **Serum 2 maps every CC to a hidden parameter of its own**: 2080 pairs, every channel × (CC 0–127, aftertouch, pitch bend), `CC14 Chan 1` and so on, the mod wheel aside. A CC knob reaches Serum's macros only after the user MIDI-learns each macro to its CC inside Serum, and that link is one-way: turning the macro in Serum never moves Stev's knob.

A probe (2026-11-01, not kept) logged `IComponentHandler::beginEdit` and `performEdit` with the parameter's title. Turning a control in either plugin's editor reported its own ID and title before any value changed: Spire's `macro1` (ID 714), Serum 2's `HIGH BOOST` (ID 7000000). So a host can learn which parameter the user means by watching them turn it, in both plugins. IDs are arbitrary numbers, never positions in the parameter list.

This is the route CLAP intends: CLAP has no way for a host to learn which parameter a CC drives (its draft `midi-mappings` extension was removed in 1.2.0), but every parameter has a stable ID, and host and plugin exchange parameter events both ways.

## Decisions (2026-11-01)

1. **The knobs drive parameters, not CCs.** The panel keeps its eight knobs in two columns of four, its place under the track headers, its sizing, and the click and dead-zone rules (`390` decision 11). Its focus and what its keys do change: decision 14. What a knob drives changes: one parameter of the selected track's plugin, by ID. The CC numbers, the CC field, the no-duplicate-CC rule and the CC 14–21 defaults go. A parameter covers what a plugin maps a CC to (a macro, a filter), whatever that plugin's MIDI mapping is. It can't cover a CC the plugin uses with no parameter behind it (a CC as a mod-matrix source): see § Out of scope.
2. **Only on a plugin track.** A MIDI Out track has no parameters. With one selected, the panel shows a one-line hint in `fg_dim` ("No plugin on this track") instead of knobs, so the panel never looks broken. The panel keeps its place, so the clip view doesn't move.
3. **Assigned by turning the control in the plugin's editor.** A right-click on a knob, its dial or its label (a two-finger tap on a trackpad), arms the knob and selects it (decision 14): the label shows a placeholder, and the footer says "Turn a control in the plugin" (`fg_dim`). The next gesture the plugin reports (VST3 `beginEdit`, CLAP a parameter gesture-begin event) assigns that parameter to the knob, and arming ends. `Esc` or a press anywhere else ends arming with nothing changed; a right-click on an assigned knob arms it to be reassigned (decision 7). An assignment is undoable, like a track's output. No context menu: with one thing to do, the right-click does it. (First built as a double-click on the label, where the CC field opened; changed 2026-11-01 after hand-testing: the label is a small target, and other hosts map a control from a right-click.) A list of the plugin's parameters to pick from is not built: plugins have hundreds of them (§ Out of scope).
4. **One parameter per knob.** Assigning a parameter another knob of the track already holds is refused with a footer hint, as `390` decision 5 refused a taken CC: both knobs would claim the same data.
5. **The label is the parameter's name.** It is shortened to fit the label's width, and hovering shows the full name in the footer. An unassigned knob shows a dim placeholder label, its dial drawn empty, and turning it does nothing. Names are read from the plugin (VST3 `getParameterInfo`, CLAP `params.get_info`) and are not saved: a load reads them again.
6. **Two-way.** The knob shows the parameter's value in force, wherever it came from: a knob drag, a turn in the plugin's editor, playback, or a preset change in the plugin. A turn in the editor of a parameter that has a knob holds that knob as a drag does (`390` decision 7, gesture-begin to gesture-end), so playback doesn't fight it. A move Stev sends is not echoed back as a user turn. This needs a guard in VST3, where `setParamNormalized` must not reach `performEdit`. Values cross to the UI thread as atomics, as the knob positions do now.
7. **The knob owns its data** (`390` decision 6, carried over). Reassigning a knob to another parameter rewrites its recorded moves to the new parameter in all of the track's clips, in the same undoable edit.
8. **A plugin swap drops the knobs.** A recorded parameter move only means something to the plugin it was recorded on. Swapping a track's plugin clears its knob assignments and every parameter move in its clips, as part of the same undoable edit: undo brings them back. Keeping them dormant until the old plugin came back would leave data nobody can see (`390` decision 6's "nothing left playing out of sight"). Changing a preset in the plugin keeps everything: the parameters are the same.
9. **A parameter never resets** (`390` decision 8). A stop, a seek or a loop wrap chases the value in force where the clip has data for it, and otherwise leaves the parameter where it was. Nothing is sent on load: the plugin's own saved state already holds every parameter, so the knobs read their positions from the plugin. Turning a knob by hand is not undoable, but it counts as a change for the unsaved prompt (`060`), as a CC knob's turn did. That holds whichever side the hand turned it from: Stev's knob, or the parameter's control in the plugin's editor. Both end in the same state, so they prompt alike. Loading a preset in the plugin counts too, knob or no knob: it is a genuine change (added 2026-11-04 after hand-testing CLAP; the prompt comes on quit, never at the preset; `060` § Unsaved changes). Each knob keeps a value for the check alone, never written to the `.stev`: the plugin's value at load, moved only by a hand turn, so playback and the chase never move it and listening to a project never asks to save (as `Track::knobs_by_hand` did). A turn in the editor of a parameter with no knob counts too (2026-11-13, issue #4): it changes the sound the project saves, as a preset does, but it keeps no value of its own and is never captured (decision 10). So does the plugin marking its own state dirty (`060` § Unsaved changes).
10. **Captured like a knob.** A knob drag, or a turn in the editor of a parameter that has a knob, goes into the capture buffer, and `370`'s landing rule applies to it per parameter, as it does to a CC (`390` decision 9). That includes the value at the turn's start, written at the window's start when the clip holds nothing earlier. A turn in the editor of a parameter with no knob is not recorded: the knob is how the user says the parameter matters. A VST3 `performEdit` carries no timing and arrives on the main thread, so it is stamped on arrival, as the computer keyboard's notes are (`090`). A CLAP output event has its sample offset in the block.
11. **Stored in the clip's event list, as a kind of its own.** A clip's events are raw MIDI today (`Event::midi_message`). A parameter move becomes an event with a payload of its own: the parameter's ID and a value normalized to 0–1 (VST3's `ParamValue`; CLAP's plain value maps through the parameter's range). Keeping it in the same list means every clip edit that moves, splits, retimes, copies, merges or deletes events carries parameter moves with no code of its own. Whatever reads MIDI (MIDI Out, `.mid` export, the MCP reads) skips them by type, never by guessing at bytes. The `.stev` gains the move kind, and an older project loads unchanged.
12. **A knob's lane draws its parameter.** 0 at the bottom, 1 at the top, drawn like the CC lanes (`390` decision 12). A stepped parameter (CLAP `is_stepped`, VST3 `stepCount` > 0) holds stepped, and its knob stops at the parameter's positions: a drag or a scroll moves it only on reaching the next one, so a take holds what the plugin heard, one clean step, not the values passed on the way (added 2026-11-04 after hand-testing a switch, whose lane drew a ramp the sound never followed). The data marker, span delete holding the value before the span (`390` decision 13), and `⌘A` then `Delete` are unchanged. (`W`'s cycle through the eight knobs went 2026-11-01: decision 14.)
13. **Old projects.** A track's saved CC knob fields are ignored on load. CC moves already recorded in clips (CC 14–21 by default) keep playing as raw MIDI. A plugin that MIDI-learned them, as Serum 2 can, still follows them. They lose their lane, which is `390` § Out of scope's "a CC no knob holds". The changelog says so.
14. **The panel's grammar: a mapping is the panel's, the data is the lane's.** A press on a knob, its dial or its label, selects it on the press, a drag after it included (a ⌘-click reset and a scroll select nothing; selecting on a dial's release, after a press that never turned it, was replaced 2026-11-01), and the panel takes the keyboard: the focus edge across the top of the panel only, as in `390` decision 14, and corner brackets around the selected knob's dial (`030` § Knob Panel; an underline under its label until 2026-11-01). The label keeps `390` decision 11's colours, `accent` while its knob's lane is up: the gutter's grammar for the lane, which the brackets don't replace, since the lane stays up after the selection goes. That works with a clip open or not: the panel shows with the clip view either way, and nothing it does needs a clip. With a clip open the click also shows the knob's lane, when the knob has a parameter (an unassigned knob has no lane to choose, so the click only selects it; a knob lane chosen earlier draws as velocity while the selected track's knob there has no parameter, `Display::shown_clip_lane`, and removing the mapping of the knob whose lane is up goes back to velocity), and drops the note selection and the duplicate span, as before. With the keyboard, `Delete` (in either pane, whatever it is bound to) **removes the selected knob's mapping**, and with it the parameter's moves in all of the track's clips (decision 8's swap, for one knob), one undo step; nothing for an unassigned knob. The footer says what went (`Removed Cutoff from knob 3`, `fg_dim`), so a `Delete` meant as a reset shows what it did, and `⌘Z` takes it back. Phase 2 adds the moves to the line. `Esc` gives the keyboard back, a pane command hands it back and acts there, and the transport, undo, the zoom and `W` work from the panel and keep it. A knob's lane is picked by the click alone: `W` cycles the gutter's VEL, PB and MW and no longer the knobs (decision 12's cycle is dropped), since a cycle through eight lanes named only in the panel is the wrong tool; from a knob's lane it goes to VEL. The selection is the panel's, stored with its focus (`KeyFocus::KnobPanel(knob)`), and a lane switch leaves it. ⌘-click turns a knob to the parameter's default (§ Phase 1 as built). Clearing a knob's recorded moves is the lane's alone: a span or `⌘A`, then `Delete` (`390` decision 13). In a take session the click only switches the lane, as before, and removing a mapping is blocked like assigning one. (Replaces `390` decision 14's `Delete`, which cleared the knob's CC from the lead clip: that made the panel's keys need a clip, and put the same clear in two places. Removing a mapping was `Delete` while armed. Changed 2026-11-01 after hand-testing.)

## How it reaches each format

- **VST3** (`180`). A knob drag or playback sets the parameter in the block's `IParameterChanges`, sample-placed for playback like clip events. The same `HostParameterChanges` already carries the CCs a plugin maps (`vst3/params.rs`). The editor follows only when the edit controller is told too: `setParamNormalized` on the main thread, so a value the audio thread sent crosses back over a ring. The editor's turns already arrive at `beginEdit` / `performEdit` / `endEdit` and cross to the processor (`vst3/params.rs`). They gain a copy to the knob and the capture buffer when the parameter has a knob. Starting values come from `getParamNormalized`.
- **CLAP** (`130`). Nothing parameter-related exists yet: `clap/host.rs` declares log, gui, state, thread-check and timer. This needs the host side of the `params` extension (`rescan`, `clear`, `request_flush`), `params.get_info` / `get_value` for names and values, parameter value input events for a knob drag and playback, reading the plugin's output events for gesture begin/end and value changes, and `params.flush` when the transport is stopped and no block runs.

## Build (phases)

Phase 1 landed ahead of phase 2, by the user's call on 2026-11-02, so knobs could be tried before they could be captured. Until phase 3, a CLAP track's panel showed the decision 2 hint, naming the format.

1. **VST3 knobs, live, two-way, and the CC knobs out.** The model (a knob's assignment by parameter ID, in `TrackData`), arming and assigning from the editor's gesture, the refusal, names on the labels, a drag setting the parameter, the knob following the editor and preset changes, the MIDI Out hint, and the swap clearing the assignments. The CC knobs' own code goes here, as it becomes dead: `models/knobs.rs`'s CC rules, the CC field, `SendKnobPositions`, and `KnobData`'s CC fields (ignored on load, decision 13). The general `Controller::Cc(n)` path stays: the mod wheel is `Cc(1)`, and a hardware CC will need it. Tests: the assignment round-trip, an old project's CC knobs ignored, a duplicate refused, an assignment undone and redone, a swap clearing the assignments and undo restoring them. Docs: `030`, `060`, `130`, `180`, `050`, `390` (superseded by this, pointer at the top).
2. **Capture and playback.** The parameter move event (decision 11), the capture buffer recording knob and editor turns, `370`'s landing rule, the chase, the hand holding a knob, reassignment rewriting the data, the swap and a removed mapping clearing the data (decision 14), and the lanes. Tests: a parameter take lands per `370` (with notes, knob-only running, knob-only stopped commits nothing), an editor turn of an unassigned parameter records nothing, clip edits carry parameter moves, MIDI Out and `.mid` export skip them, a seek chases the value, a hand turn from Stev or the editor counts for the unsaved prompt while playback doesn't. Docs: `090`, `370`, `380`, `050`, `060`. Changelog, for both phases: the knobs drive a plugin parameter, picked by turning it in the plugin, captured with `/` whether turned in Stev or in the plugin; the CC knobs are gone, with what happens to old projects (decision 13).
3. **CLAP.** The `params` extension and events of § How it reaches each format, behind the same knob interface, so nothing above the plugin host changes. Tests: the plain/normalized mapping and the output-event parsing, both pure. Docs: `130`, and `390` archived once its kept rules are folded into `030`/`380`. Changelog: parameter knobs work with CLAP plugins. The commit that finishes this phase closes #16.

### Phase 1 as built (2026-11-01)

The model is `models/knobs.rs` (`ParamId`, `KnobParams`, `param_taken_by`), the edit `SetKnobParamEdit`, the plugin side `InstrumentEditor`'s parameter methods (`130` § What it does, `180` § Parameter knobs), the view's link `view/display/knob_params.rs`. Choices the decisions left open:

- **Values are read from the plugin, not mirrored.** The panel reads `getParamNormalized` straight off the edit controller when it draws; `TrackMixAtomics::knob_value` went with the CC knobs. Phase 2 keeps this: playback tells the controller instead (§ Phase 2 as built).
- **The hand's value for the unsaved check starts unset on a load**, not at the plugin's value (decision 9): a hand turn sets it, so turning a knob back exactly where it was still asks to save. Reading every assigned parameter at load to seed it wasn't worth it for a float the hand never lands on twice.
- **⌘-click goes to the parameter's default** (VST3 `defaultNormalizedValue`), not 0: a parameter's rest is the plugin's to say, and 0 is wrong for a centred pan or a tuning.
- **Labels**: an unassigned knob reads `Knob 3` in `fg_dim`, an armed one `Turn…` in `accent`; hovering an unassigned knob's label or dial says how to assign it in the footer. The hint on a CLAP track read `No knobs for CLAP plugins yet` until phase 3.
- **The computer keyboard's router records notes and wheels only**: `MidiInputForwarder::knob_router` and `is_recorded`'s any-controller switch went with the CC route.
- **An assignment is already blocked by a take session**, with the session's footer message, since phase 2's data rewrite will touch the take's clip.

### Phase 2 as built (2026-11-04)

A move is `ParamMove { param, value }`, `param` a `PluginParam`: the ID with its plugin's tag (`plugin_tag`, a hash of the plugin's id). It is an `Event` payload beside the MIDI bytes (`models/event.rs`); `Event::midi_message` is empty for one, so nothing reading MIDI recognises it. `Controller` gains `Param(PluginParam)`, so the landing rule, the chase, the lanes and the span delete are the wheels' code, and `Event::controller_move` reads any controller from an event. Choices the decisions left open:

- **Controller values became `f64`, the per-controller arrays small sorted lists.** A parameter's ID is arbitrary and its value normalized, so `ControllerValues` and `ControllerTracker` no longer index by controller, and `Controller::index` and `COUNT` went. `Controller::rest`, `parse_controller` and `Controller::message` stay in MIDI's `u16`. A lane keeps its `u16` too: a parameter is scaled to `PARAM_LANE_MAX` in the `ControllerMoves` snapshot, 16 bits being more than a lane has pixels.
- **The editor follows from the sequencer thread, not back from the audio thread.** The design had a played value cross back over a ring. It needs no ring: the sequencer knows the value when it sends it, so `tick` leaves it in `TrackMixAtomics::knob_played` (decision 6's atomics) and `Display` tells the edit controller once a frame.
- **A turn is three commands** (`HoldKnob`, `SetKnobValue`, `ReleaseKnob`), the same for all three hands: a drag (from its first step past the dead zone), a scroll, and the editor's gesture. A scroll has no release, so its turn ends once `WHEEL_TURN_HOLD` has passed with no scroll (`end_finished_wheel_turn`). A ⌘-click is a turn of one move, recorded like any other.
- **Only a held knob's moves are captured.** A `performEdit` outside `beginEdit`/`endEdit` is the plugin's own, a preset it loaded: it counts for the unsaved prompt, as in phase 1, but no take keeps it. A plugin that reports a hand turn with no gesture around it can't be captured.
- **Only the selected track's turns are captured.** The buffer is one, and `/` lands it on the selected track, so a turn on another track's plugin stays out. A commit also drops any move no knob of the selected track holds by then (`Sequencer::keep_knob_moves`).
- **A parameter no knob of the track holds plays nothing** (`Track::plays`). Clips can carry moves to a track with another plugin (a paste, a move between tracks), where the ID means something else or nothing. They are left in the clip, unheard and with no lane, and play again if the clip comes back. Deleting them on the way in would make a paste destroy data; playing them would turn the wrong control.
- **A parameter is its plugin's, not just its ID.** A knob holds a `PluginParam`, the plugin's tag beside the ID, and every comparison of a move with a knob compares both, so a pasted clip whose ID happens to match a knob stays silent, draws in no lane, and isn't touched by that knob's reassignment or removal. The tag sits in the type rather than in a check, so playback, the chase, the lanes and the rewrites need no rule of their own. The ID alone is enough where the plugin itself is talking (its gestures, `param_taken_by`): a track's knobs are all its own plugin's. On the clip rather than on each move would have been smaller, but a merge or a take landing on a pasted clip would then mix two plugins' moves under one owner. (Added 2026-11-04, from the `/simplify` review.)
- **The footer's line comes back from the sequencer** (`UiEvent::KnobMappingRemoved`): whether moves went is known there, across all the track's clips. It reads `Removed Cutoff and its moves from knob 3`.
- **A stepped knob** stops where VST3 lays the positions out (`snap_to_steps`: `stepCount + 1` equal shares of the range, so a switch flips at the middle, not at rounding's halfway points). The drag and the scroll keep the raw travel beside the value shown, so a slow turn still reaches the next position. A plugin that reports no steps for a switch turns smoothly: Stev can't tell.
- **A stepped lane** skips `ease_steps`, so it keeps `wheel_path`'s flat holds and vertical steps, where a smooth parameter's line joins move to move.
- **A held parameter's chase is dropped, not deferred.** On release playback has it back from the clip's next move or chase, as a CC knob did.
- **`endEdit` is trusted.** A plugin that never sends it leaves the knob held until the track's knobs change. The probe logged `beginEdit` and `performEdit` only. Spire and Serum 2 both send it (hand-tested 2026-11-04: a control dragged in each plugin's window plays back).

### Phase 3 as built (2026-11-04)

Hand-tested 2026-11-04 in a CLAP plugin. `clap/params.rs` is the whole of it: `ParamRange` maps plain to normalized and back, `ParamValues` reads what the plugin reports, and `bridge` builds the editor's and the voice's ends. `ParameterInfo`'s `steps` is the range's width for an `IS_STEPPED` parameter. Above the plugin host nothing changed but the hint. Choices the design left open:

- **No `params.flush`.** § How it reaches each format wanted one for when no block runs, but a block always can: the engine's callback never stops, and only a sleeping voice skips `process`. So `request_flush` is `request_process`, a knob's move waiting in the ring wakes the voice, and `process` does what a flush would. It also keeps the host from calling the plugin's `flush` from the audio thread beside `process`, which it may not do at once.
- **The editor keeps every value** (`ParamValues`), where VST3 reads the controller. `get_value` needs a `&mut` handle and `parameter_value` is `&self`, and a turn's start can't be read when the panel hears of it: a report crosses from `process`, and the plugin has moved on by then. Reports are read in order, so the start is the value kept from before them. The cache follows the reports, Stev's sends, playback (`follow_parameter`, which is all CLAP needs: the plugin updates its own editor) and a rescan.
- **A rescan of the values is one `ParameterGesture::ValuesChanged`**, as VST3's `restartComponent` now is: the kept values are read again, the knobs follow, the unsaved prompt counts it, and no take keeps it. `rescan` only marks it (`values_rescan`), since the plugin is inside its own call then; `take_parameter_gestures` reads it.
- **A value that changes nothing is no move.** CLAP has no call to fence off as VST3's `setParamNormalized` is, and a plugin shouldn't report what the host sent it. One that does repeats the value already kept, which reads as nothing. A plugin that repeats an older value while a drag runs past it would still read as a move.
- **`CLAP_EVENT_DONT_RECORD` moves the knob and nothing else**: the plugin's own MIDI mapping turned the control, so it neither prompts nor records.
- **Output events never allocate.** `ReportFeed` is the `OutputEventBuffer`: it keeps what is about a parameter and pushes it on a ring from the plugin's own `try_push`, and never refuses an event. A turn is stamped at the block's start plus its sample offset (`event_nanos`).
- **The audio thread wakes the UI through the `"plugin-host"` thread** (`UiWake`): an editor turn reaches Stev only in `process`, and `request_repaint` takes a lock. Without it a stopped transport would leave the knob still until something else repainted.
- **Ranges and names are read once**, before activation, after the state is restored. CLAP only changes a range under `rescan(ALL)`, while deactivated, and `request_restart` stays ignored. A `rescan(INFO)` keeps the old names until a reload, as VST3 does, and `clear` is ignored: the knob and its moves stay, with no name.
- **A plugin with no `params` extension, or no parameters**, shows `This plugin has no parameters`: decision 2's hint. The trait's parameter methods lost their do-nothing defaults, now both formats have them.

## Out of scope

- **A CC as a knob's target.** For a CC with no parameter behind it, such as a mod-matrix CC source, or for external gear on a MIDI Out track. In VST3 it costs no new audio path: Stev already sends a CC by looking it up in `IMidiMapping` and writing the hidden parameter (Serum 2's `CC20 Chan 1`), so a CC target is a parameter target found through the table. It belongs with the hardware controllers.
- **Hardware controllers.** A converter from a hardware encoder to knob *n* of the selected track (`390` § Out of scope), which now reaches parameters through these knobs.
- **A list of the plugin's parameters to pick from.** Searchable if it comes: a large synth has hundreds of parameters, and Serum 2 has 2080 hidden CC parameters on top.
- **Recording every parameter turned in the editor**, with or without a knob. Decision 10 records only the knobs' parameters.
- **Undoing a hand turn** of a knob, in Stev or in the plugin's editor (decision 9). An editor turn can't be made undoable without Stev reaching into the plugin, which often has an undo of its own, and making only Stev's turn undoable would make `⌘Z` depend on which control the hand used. Knobs are noodled with, so the turns would also bury real edits in the undo history. A capture with `/` is how a turn is kept, and that can be undone.
- **More than eight knobs**, keyboard control, and drawing parameter data (`280` § Not planned).
- **MCP writes of parameter moves** (`360`).
